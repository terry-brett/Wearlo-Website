Frequently Asked Questions

How do presence tiers and "Lock when I'm…" work?

Instead of relying on raw meter estimates, Wearlo compares your watch's Bluetooth signal against your computer's Reference Signal and shows one of three tiers: Near Mac / Near PC (green), Nearby (yellow), or Away (red). While connected, only Away triggers a proximity lock - Near Mac / Near PC and Nearby never lock on their own, and returning to either cancels a pending lock countdown.

The Lock when I'm… slider on the dashboard controls how much weaker the signal has to get before Nearby turns into Away and locks:

  • Stepping away (~2 m): Switches from Nearby to Away soon after you leave your computer.
  • Across the room (~4 m, default): Stays in Nearby as you move around close by, then switches to Away across the room.
  • Far away (~8 m): Keeps you in Nearby across a wider area and only switches to Away much farther away.
  • Out of range: Disables the Away tier and locks only when the Bluetooth connection drops.

Your Reference Signal is captured automatically when you pair and keeps adapting while you actively use your computer, or you can click Recalibrate in Settings at any time.

How do "Safe Networks" work?

Wearlo allows you to designate specific WiFi networks (such as your home network) as Safe Networks. When your computer is connected to one of these networks, the proximity auto-lock feature is completely bypassed - your computer will stay unlocked even if you walk away or your watch disconnects. Note for Mac users: Due to macOS security requirements, you must grant Wearlo "Location Services" permissions for this feature to work.

What is the Lock Delay setting?

The Lock Delay gives you a customizable grace period (between 0 and 60 seconds, defaulting to 3 seconds) before your computer locks after your watch enters the Away tier (or disconnects when Lock on Disconnect is enabled). If your watch moves back to Nearby or Near Mac / Near PC before the timer expires, the lock is canceled. This prevents accidental locking caused by brief Bluetooth signal dips.

How does automatic screen wake work?

With Wake display when I come back enabled in Settings (on by default), Wearlo automatically turns your computer's display on when the screen is locked and your watch moves closer again (for example, returning from Away to Nearby or Near Mac / Near PC), so your Windows Hello camera or Mac login screen is ready for you to sign in.

What happens if the watch battery dies or disconnects?

If Lock on Disconnect is off (the default unless you select Out of range on the dashboard slider), a sudden Bluetooth drop from right next to your computer will not lock it. Turning on Lock on Disconnect (or choosing Out of range) locks your computer after the connection is lost. On Windows, if your watch was already Nearby or Away before disconnecting, it also starts the walk-away lock countdown. Wearlo will never trigger an auto-lock on a fresh launch unless the watch has connected and verified at least once during that session.

Can the desktop app pair with multiple watches?

No. The Wearlo desktop app's secure storage system is designed to retain the cryptographic pairing credentials for exactly one watch at a time. If you pair a new watch, it will securely overwrite the previous watch's data. However, a single watch can be paired to multiple different computers, as the watch stores a unique Identifier for each computer it pairs with.

Windows

Why isn't my PC waking up or reconnecting when I walk up to it?

When your PC idles, Windows power management may suspend background apps and power down the Bluetooth adapter. To keep Bluetooth active while locked, the recommended option in Settings is to set Windows Power & Sleep ("When plugged in, put my device to sleep after") to Never. If you can't change that system setting or are on battery, you can enable the Keep Awake Timer slider in Settings instead to keep the PC running for a set duration while allowing the screen to turn off.

My PC locked while I was sitting right in front of it. How do I fix this?

Wearlo automatically pauses locking while you're actively using your keyboard or mouse and adapts your Reference Signal to your normal desk posture. If your PC still locks when you sit still - for example, if your body or desk blocks the Bluetooth signal - click Recalibrate in Settings and stay at your computer for 10 seconds, choose a less sensitive stop on Lock when I'm… (such as Across the room or Far away), or increase Lock Delay.
